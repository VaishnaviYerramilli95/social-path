# SocialPilot – End-User Guide

Welcome to **SocialPilot**! This user guide outlines the step-by-step workflow to manage campaigns and publish posts across platforms.

---

## 🔑 1. User Access & Seeding

1. Access the portal at `http://localhost:3000`.
2. Click **Create an Account** to navigate to the Registration screen.
3. Input your name, email, phone, company, and password, then click **Register**.
4. **Auto-seeding Active**: Upon your first login, the platform will automatically populate your dashboard with mock social profiles, demo marketing campaigns, post queues, and analytics datasets.

---

## 👥 2. Connect Social Channels

1. Select **Social Accounts** from the left navigation sidebar.
2. Under **Add Social Channel**, click the platform logo you wish to connect (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, or Twitter/X).
3. In the simulated authorization modal, type in your handle name (e.g. `@my_business_page`) and click **Authorize & Link**.
4. Your connected profiles will now display on your dashboard.

---

## 📅 3. Schedule Content Updates

1. Select **Scheduler** from the sidebar navigation.
2. Click the calendar view to check dates. Double-click any day or click the **Schedule Post** button.
3. Choose the target **Platform**, type your **Caption**, and add a **Media URL** if applicable.
4. Input your desired **Date** and **Time**, choose a recurring option (none, weekly, monthly), and click **Schedule Post**.
5. Your post will now appear in your calendar queue.

---

## 📣 4. Manage Campaigns

1. Select **Campaigns** from the sidebar.
2. Click **+ New Campaign**.
3. Name your campaign (e.g. "Winter Discount Drive"), select a primary platform, choose start/end dates, set a budget, and describe your objective.
4. Click **Create Campaign**.
5. Posts created in the scheduler can be linked directly to your active campaigns.

---

## 📈 5. Analyze Growth & Export Reports

1. Select **Analytics** to view REACH, followers growth, CTR, and ROI statistics.
2. Click **Export PDF Report** to print a hardcopy.
3. To download spreadsheet raw logs, select **Reports** from the navigation bar, filter by date range, platform, or campaign, and click **Export CSV** (for either Campaigns or Post lists).
